Account

Delete your Traverse account

You can delete your Traverse account and the personal information attached to it at any time — from inside the app, or from this page if you can no longer sign in.

From the app

Open Profile, scroll to Delete my account, and confirm. The account is closed straight away.

From this page

Use the form below. It asks for the phone number on the account and sends a code to it, so that nobody else can close your account for you.

Open the deletion form

What deleting does

  • Your name, phone number and email are removed from the account.
  • You are signed out everywhere and cannot sign in again.
  • Saved places, saved routes and your referral code are deleted.
  • Records of rides and money — trips, wallet entries, payments — are kept without your personal details in them. Ghanaian tax and accounting rules require a business to keep its financial records, and a driver's earnings cannot be reconstructed if the rides are erased. Nothing in those records names you afterwards.

If something goes wrong

Email traversegh@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will close it for you.
